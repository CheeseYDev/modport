04 / STAFF APPROACH

Sound judgement is
part of the job.

How I approach the situations that matter when working with members and other staff.

Disagreeing with staff

I keep disagreements respectful and private. I explain my reasoning, listen to the other perspective and focus on the rule, evidence and impact rather than making it personal.

Making difficult decisions

I gather the relevant context, check the rules and consider the consequences. Then I choose a fair, proportionate response and make sure the reasoning is clear.

Taking responsibility

If I make a mistake, I acknowledge it, correct what I can and learn from it. Protecting my ego should never come before the community.

Handling criticism

I stay open to feedback, ask questions when something is unclear and separate useful criticism from personal frustration.

Confidentiality and trust

Reports, tickets, staff discussions and private information stay private. Access and authority are responsibilities, not privileges to misuse.

Working as one team

I respect role boundaries, communicate handovers and support a consistent staff standard. If an issue exceeds my authority, I escalate it appropriately.

My standard: Be fair. Be respectful. Be accountable. Put the community first.
