HOW I WORK

Trust is built through
every decision.

Tools can be learned. Good judgement, respect and accountability are what make someone dependable on a staff team.

01

Disagreements stay professional.

I discuss differences privately, listen to other perspectives and focus on facts, rules and what is best for the community.

02

Decisions are reasoned, not personal.

I consider context, evidence, consistency and impact before choosing a fair and proportionate response.

03

Responsibility includes owning mistakes.

I can accept feedback, acknowledge an error and correct it. Staff authority should never become an excuse for ego.

04

Confidentiality is non-negotiable.

Reports, tickets and internal staff matters stay private and are shared only with people who need to know.
