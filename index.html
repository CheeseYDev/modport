STAFF APPROACH

Good judgement
matters.

Tools are only part of being good staff. The way you communicate, make decisions and treat people matters just as much.

01

When I disagree with another staff member

I listen first, discuss the issue privately and explain my reasoning with facts and server rules. I focus on solving the problem, not winning an argument. If needed, I use the proper escalation route.

02

How I make decisions

I consider the rules, evidence, context, impact and consistency. I avoid acting on assumptions, and I ask for a second opinion when a decision is unclear or outside my authority.

03

When I make a mistake

I take responsibility, correct what I can and communicate honestly with the people involved. Being staff does not mean being right all the time.

04

How I handle trust and confidential information

Reports, staff discussions and private member information stay private. I use permissions only for their intended purpose and never let personal relationships influence decisions.

05

How I respond to feedback

I take feedback professionally, ask questions when something is unclear and use it to improve. Disagreement is not automatically disrespect.
